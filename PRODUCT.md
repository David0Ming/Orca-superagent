# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The owner manages work across projects inside Orca, often while other agents are working asynchronously.

## Product Purpose

Make requests, questions, progress and outcomes traceable and actionable without opening every agent window.

## Brand Personality

Direct, quiet and trustworthy. Use the language of the work, with honest distinctions between saved, delivered, read and processed.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy health dashboards, invented status, and controls that imply capabilities the system does not have.

## Design Principles

- Lead with what needs the owner.
- Keep project tasks and their conversations together.
- Preserve original words and unsent drafts.
- Separate business progress from native execution state.
- Use Orca's native components and conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-accessible native controls, visible text for states, and host light and dark themes.
